Customer Understanding

Target Segment & The Core Purchase Decision

Target Persona
👤 Primary Consumer Persona [Assumption A001]

Demographic: Urban Indian women aged 25–45 investing in preventative wellness, skin elasticity, hair/nail strengthening, and joint recovery.

Device Usage: Estimated 70%+ Mobile-first shopping behavior, driven by Instagram/Meta social ad discovery.

Mindset: Health-conscious, label-literate, but skeptical of unverified nutraceutical claims and suspicious of unpleasant fishy odor/aftertaste.

The Core Friction Question

"Will this Korean Marine Collagen deliver visible anti-aging results, and is the brand trustworthy enough to spend ₹1,400+ on a first-time purchase?"

⚠️ Dual Decision Barriers
  • Efficacy & Taste Hesitation: Fear of wasted money on synthetic fillers or fishy taste.
  • Price & Risk Anxiety: ₹1,745 entry cost without upfront shipping SLAs, COD assurance, or clear money-back terms.

F007: Mobile CTA Below Fold [Observed]

On standard mobile screens (390×844), the Add-to-Cart (ATC) button is buried beneath extensive imagery. Users who don't scroll deeply never see the primary action button.

Impact: High drop-off before cart initiation
F005: Absent Delivery SLAs [Observed]

No shipping cost threshold, estimated delivery dates, or COD availability are visible near the purchase decision zone. Users must navigate to checkout to discover total cost.

Impact: Checkout abandonment due to cost anxiety
F008: Multi-Pack Savings Flaw [Pricing Bug]

Original site offers only a flat ~15% discount across 1, 2, and 3 packs, offering zero progressive incentive for bulk purchases. Furthermore, the 4-pack bundle is listed at ₹1 MORE than buying 4 individual items!

Impact: Suppresses AOV & damages buyer trust

Primary Intervention

Single Priority Choice: Sticky Mobile Add-to-Cart Bar

Rank #1 Focus
💡 The Core Solution Architecture

A fixed bottom bar that appears dynamically once a mobile user scrolls past the primary hero gallery (300px threshold).

  • Live Price Display: Reflects active pack selection (₹1,745).
  • One-Tap Purchasing: High-contrast green CTA opening cart drawer.
  • Safe-Zone Clearance: Floats above navigation without covering chat widgets.
📊 Why It Won Highest Priority (8/9)
1. Direct Funnel Impact: Targets the single highest drop-off touchpoint for 70%+ of mobile shoppers.
2. Zero Backend Risk: 100% client-side CSS/JS implementation; no database or ERP changes required.

Experimentation Framework

Controlled A/B Testing Specifications

Zero Vanity Lifts
EXP-001: Sticky Mobile ATC Bar

Hypothesis: A persistent bottom ATC bar will increase mobile Add-to-Cart rate by eliminating scroll friction.

Control: Standard inline CTA requiring multi-screen scrolling.

Variant: Fixed bottom bar appearing after 300px scroll.

Primary Metric: Mobile Add-to-Cart Rate
Guardrail Metrics: Bounce Rate & Average Order Value (AOV)

Audience: 100% Mobile traffic (50/50 split).

EXP-002: Shipping & Multi-Pack Offers

Hypothesis: Transparent shipping SLAs combined with progressive multi-pack savings (32% → 36%) will increase checkout transitions and AOV.

Control: Flat 15% discount & zero shipping info near CTA.

Variant: 3-icon SLA strip + clear multi-pack savings pills.

Primary Metric: Product-Page-to-Checkout Rate
Guardrail Metric: Return & Refund Rate

Audience: All devices; segmented by new vs. returning.

Execution Strategy

Controlled A/B Testing

100% Rollout & Expansion
  • Promote winning variant to 100% mobile production traffic.

  • Incorporate learnings into other SKU templates.
